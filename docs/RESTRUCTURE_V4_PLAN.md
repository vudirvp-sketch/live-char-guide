## 1. Principle

**One place, on time.** Every fact (rule, number, example, check) is printed exactly once — in the chapter
where the reader applies it. A rule needed earlier is published earlier and not repeated later; a rule needed
later is published there and absent earlier.

Corollary: a link may only point to a *different level* (appendix / reference), never to the definition of
what is being used right now. Local reminders are allowed and capped at one sentence (DEC-15).

Why: v3/v4 measurements show literal duplication is exhausted (4 near-dup paragraph pairs; 270 cross-refs).
The residual load is architectural — explanation and application sit in different Parts, so the reader walks.

## 4. Constraints (do not break)

- **Ids stay.** Section ids are frozen for v4 (anchor economy: 128 redirects + 45 glossary anchors + 7 gates).
  A chapter may move sections between files without renaming them; the TOC takes the section's own heading.
  Renumbering is a separate, owner-approved iteration if ever wanted.
- **Canon-first.** Every edit lands in `docs/canon/part_NN.md` and `src/master/part_NN.html` in the same step;
  `audit_canon_master_sync.py` MUST stay at 87/87 (re-point probes when a locked sentence is deliberately retired).
- **Parity gates stay strict.** E01/E07/E08/E09/E10/E11/E12/E13/E14/E16/E17 remain the single visual
  presentation of their concept; a chapter owns the prose, the embed stays where its gate locks it.
- **Editorial Policy (DEC-15)** — compress redundant presentation, never unique capability; UNCLEAR ≠ delete.
- **Merges retire ids to legacy redirects (owner call, iter 188 — DEC-33).** One new `data/anchor-redirects.json` entry per absorbed section, every target live (`SHELL-REDIRECTS`), no chains, no shadowing; glossary `home`/`xrefs` and the canon § numbers re-pointed in the same step. Never drop an id without its redirect.

- **Reader-facing numbers** come from the single owner (§7A.12 budgets, §5.1 OCEAN ladder, §3.2 voice matrix):
  a chapter may state the one number it needs; it may not restate the table.
